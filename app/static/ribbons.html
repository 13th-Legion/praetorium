Tier 4 — Years of Service

Anni Stipendiorum

Plain tenure discs (not ribbons). Greedy fill from TSM join date — gold = years÷5, then silver = remainder÷3, then bronze. e.g. 8 yrs = 1 gold + 1 bronze = 18 pts.

DiscRepresentsPoints each
● Gold5 years15
● Silver3 years9
● Bronze1 year3
How points roll up: Promotions page (/api/s1/promotions) shows two totals per member — Lifetime (sum of everything) and Since last promotion (awards dated after the last promo). Tiered ribbons store base + per-device increment so devices sum correctly. Decorations and tenure discs both count toward the totals. All numbers here are proposed — tune before locking into the catalog.
