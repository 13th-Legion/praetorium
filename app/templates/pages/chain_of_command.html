CO Board (Governing) All Company Commanders Parallel with RCO Board
Each unit is a company commanded by a Captain. In normal operations there is no single overall commander — the CO Board (all Company Commanders) governs in parallel with the RCO Board as checks-and-balances, and the CSM serves as senior enlisted advisor.

As TSM grows, companies are grouped into Regions (recommended ~3 companies each; always an odd number of regions, minimum 3), each led by a Regional Commander (Major). The Majors form the RCO Board.

In an emergency, if the CO Board deems it necessary to have an overall Officer in Charge, the Regional Commanders elect a temporary State Commander from among themselves — honorary rank of Colonel — to singularly lead TSM. (Bylaws §2.1.2–§2.1.3)
