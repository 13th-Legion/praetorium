FTX Course of Fire

Texas State Militia — 13th Legion

Range Day Reference — Phases, Procedures & Ammunition Requirements

1. General Information

The shoot will include 3 RSOs and 1 RO. Maximum of 5 shooters on the line at a time. Members should bring a shooting mat and have their magazines marked with a name or symbol. Minimum magazine count: 5 rifle, 3 pistol.

Every attending member will go through the reloading, malfunction, and field stripping class. The goal is to have lower-skilled shooters complete the class prior to moving on to the second shooting phase of the day. Class size is variable. Recommend an experienced shooter to instruct this section to speed up completion.

An armory table will be set up with all ammunition and magazines. A minimum of two S4 members will man the armory at all times to maintain accurate round counts and controlled distribution.

EQUIPMENT CHECK: Ensure all shooters have proper eye and ear protection, serviceable weapons, and required magazine counts before proceeding to the line. Magazines should be clearly marked for identification.

3. Warm-Up / Knowledge Check

AmmunitionMagazinesPosition
4 rounds (rifle)1 magazineStanding → Kneeling → Sitting → Prone

All shooters will line up in equal lines based on RSO count. One magazine with 4 rounds will be placed in their kit or hand. Upon approaching the line, shooters will be given the command to load and make ready.

  1. At the RO's command of "CONTACT" — disengage safety, fire one round standing, engage safety.
  2. Kneel — disengage safety, fire one round, engage safety.
  3. Sit — disengage safety, fire one round, engage safety.
  4. Prone — disengage safety, fire one round, drop magazine.
  5. Show firearm clear, bolt locked back. Move off the firing line.
ASSESSMENT: After this shoot, non-patched members and shooters who demonstrated insufficient weapons handling will be sent to the reloading, malfunction clearing, and basic field stripping class before proceeding to subsequent phases.

4. Malfunction / Reload Shoot

AmmunitionMagazinesNotes
19 live rounds + 2 dummy rounds3 magazines (7 rounds each)Dummy rounds in 2nd & 3rd mag, random position (not first round)

The shooter will approach the line. The first magazine to be loaded will be noted verbally upon pickup from the reloading table. The two spare magazines will be on the shooter's person.

Shooters will be allowed to get prepared for the command to fire.

At the "CONTACT" command, shooters will fire all rounds on target. Shooters are expected to reload their rifles and clear malfunctions as encountered.

MALFUNCTION HANDLING: Dummy rounds are deliberately placed to induce malfunctions. Shooters must demonstrate proper immediate action (tap-rack-bang) and remedial action procedures. RSOs will observe and assess each shooter's response.

5. Transition Shoot

AmmunitionMagazinesDistance
10 rounds rifle + 5 rounds pistol1 rifle mag, 1 pistol mag15-yard line

Magazines with the correct round count (10 rifle and 5 pistol) will be pre-loaded for the shooter. Shooters are not to know how many rounds are in the magazines.

At the "CONTACT" command, the shooter will fire all 15 rounds into the target, transitioning from rifle to pistol when the rifle runs dry.

6. Shooting While Moving

Rifle

AmmunitionMagazinesDistance
20 rounds2 magazines (10 rounds each)25-yard → 7-yard line

Shooters will line up on the 25-yard line and aim at the target. At the command of "MOVE", shooters will walk on-line even with one another towards the target. At the command of "CONTACT" (while walking), the shooter will fire one round. Shooters will stop walking on the 7-yard line.

All rounds not fired by the 7-yard line will be fired at the 7-yard line.

Pistol

AmmunitionMagazinesDistance
20 rounds2 magazines (10 rounds each)15-yard → 5-yard line

Shooters will line up on the 15-yard line and aim at the target. At the command of "MOVE", shooters will walk on-line even with one another towards the target. At the command of "CONTACT" (while walking), the shooter will fire one round. Shooters will stop walking on the 5-yard line.

All rounds not fired by the 5-yard line will be fired at the 5-yard line.

7. Lateral Movement Shoot (Walking Side-to-Side)

AmmunitionMagazinesTargets
10 rounds rifle + 10 rounds pistol1 rifle mag, 1 pistol mag5 targets numbered 1–5

One shooter at a time. Everyone can be in line waiting their turn. This drill covers both rifle and pistol.

Five targets will be set up with large numbering: 1, 2, 3, 4, and 5.

Rifle

At the command of "WALK", the shooter will walk across the line from left-to-right or right-to-left. The RO will call out target numbers. The shooter will fire two rounds at the designated target when the number is called — regardless of which target they are currently in front of.

Pistol

After 10 rounds from the rifle, the shooter will show clear, then sling the rifle. The shooter will draw their pistol. Upon the "WALK" command, the shooter will walk the opposite direction back toward the starting point, firing two rounds at each called number while walking.

8. Audible — Wedge Shoot (Rifle Only)

AmmunitionMagazinesShootersDistance
2 rounds per shooter1 magazine each3 shooters25-yard line
SAFETY CRITICAL: This is a complicated movement drill and MUST BE PRACTICED UNLOADED NUMEROUS TIMES prior to attempting with live ammunition. All shooters must demonstrate proficiency in dry runs before the RO authorizes live fire.

While in the wedge formation at the 25-yard line, the command to "WALK" will be given. All shooters will move together in a wedge formation.

  1. The point man fires two rounds, then stops in his tracks.
  2. The shooter to the right of the point man steps forward to the point position and fires two rounds. The previous point man moves to the right side of the wedge.
  3. The new point man stops after firing. The shooter to the left steps forward to become point man and fires two rounds. The previous point man moves to the left side of the formation.

All shooters must stay together and always move in formation. Muzzle discipline and awareness of adjacent shooters is paramount.

9. Rifle Qualification

Total RoundsMagazinesPassing Score
50 rounds5 magazines (10 rounds each)35 hits on target
MAGAZINE PREP: 5 magazines, each loaded with 10 rounds. Shooters should have all magazines on their person or staged at their position before beginning.

Stage 1 — 10-Yard Line

DistanceRoundsMagazinesPosition
10 yards10 rounds1 magazine (10 rounds)Standing

At the call of "CONTACT", the shooter will fire 10 rounds standing.

Stage 2 — 25-Yard Line

DistanceRoundsMagazinesPosition
25 yards10 rounds1 magazine (10 rounds)Standing → Kneeling

At the call of "CONTACT", the shooter will fire 5 rounds standing, then 5 rounds kneeling.

Stage 3 — 50-Yard Line

DistanceRoundsMagazinesPositionTime Limit
50 yards30 rounds3 magazines (10 rounds each)Standing → Kneeling → Prone2 minutes

At the call of "CONTACT", the shooter will fire 10 rounds standing, then 10 rounds kneeling, then 10 rounds prone. Time limit is 2 minutes.

SCORING: 35 hits on target out of 50 rounds to pass. Targets will be scored after all three stages are complete.

10. Competition Shoot

AmmunitionMagazinesScoring
50 rounds rifle + 5 rounds pistol5 rifle mags (10 rds each) + 1 pistol magHits on target + time to complete

One shooter at a time. No induced malfunctions (dummy rounds), but reloads are expected between each stage. Shooters may reload on the move or at the new position.

Course of Fire

  1. 100 meters — Fire 10 rounds prone. Advance to 80 meters.
  2. 80 meters — Reload. Fire 10 rounds standing. Advance to 60 meters.
  3. 60 meters — Reload. Fire 10 rounds kneeling. Advance to 40 meters.
  4. 40 meters — Reload. Fire 10 rounds seated. Crawl to 30 meters.
  5. 30 meters — Reload. Fire 10 rounds on the waterfall (standing → kneeling → sitting → prone). Advance to 20 meters.
  6. 20 meters — Sling rifle. Draw pistol. Fire 5 rounds while walking forward to 10 meters.
SAFETY: Shooters will NOT draw their sidearm prior to the 20-meter line. Rifle must be slung before transitioning to pistol.

11. Night / Low Light Shoot

Total RoundsMagazinesEquipmentPassing Score
60 rounds (rifle)2 mags × 30 rds or 3 mags totaling 60 rdsWeapon light or flashlight required42 hits on target

Stage 1 — 7-Yard Line

At the command of "CONTACT", fire 10 rounds standing.

Stage 2 — 10-Yard Line

Shooters move as a group to the 10-yard line. At the command of "CONTACT", fire 10 rounds kneeling, then 10 rounds standing.

Stage 3 — 15-Yard Line

Shooters move as a group to the 15-yard line. At the command of "CONTACT", shooters will reload their rifle, then fire 10 rounds standing, then 10 rounds kneeling.

Stage 4 — 25-Yard Line

Shooters move as a group to the 25-yard line. At the command of "CONTACT", fire 5 rounds standing, then 5 rounds prone.
