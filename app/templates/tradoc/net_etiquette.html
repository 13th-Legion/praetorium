Net Etiquette

13th Legion — Training Reference

Comms Basics · Training Block 3 · 2026

This is how you talk once you have the station. Who is on which net, and how you keep it secure, is Radio Familiarity. What you send is Reports.

Prowords are short standard words with one agreed meaning, used across all units. They make traffic readable and they cut down on "say again" loops. The standard for the words you choose is the 3 C's: Clear, Concise, Correct.

1. Prowords

ProwordMeaning
BREAKPause. More of the same message is coming. Unkey while you pause.
OVERI am done talking, and I am expecting a response.
OUTEnd of the transmission. I am done talking. Do not respond.
CORRECTIONCorrects a misheard message.
ROGERConfirms receipt of the message.
SAY AGAINAsk the sender to repeat the last transmission.
NEVER SAY "REPEAT". Say again. And never say "over and out." Over asks for an answer. Out says do not answer. Together they contradict each other, and the block calls it redundant for that reason.

3. Establishing contact

The call is: "Hey YOU, this is ME, over." Their callsign, then yours, then over.

  • Do not relay the information until they answer. Contact first. The report second.
  • No answer: wait 30 seconds and try again.
  • Still no answer: wait 2 minutes and try again.
  • If that fails, stop mashing the button. Troubleshoot the radio — line of sight and terrain, antenna height, foliage, transmit power. That checklist is in Radio Familiarity.

4. Running the conversation

You sayYou mean
OverExpecting a response. Stop talking.
BreakMore information follows. Unkey for each break.
OutEnd of the conversation. They do not answer.
EIGHT SECONDS. A transmission should be no more than 8 continuous seconds. That is why a LACE report is sent as Lima, break, Alpha, break — not as one breath. Unkey on the break.
